## Selected work

### QA-Robot

Self-hosted governance for AI-generated tests. A QA tool that executes AI
output is a remote code execution engine with extra steps, so QA-Robot scans
generated tests for credential theft, data exfiltration and production side
effects before they run, then keeps an append-only record of what executed.

Everything stays on your own hardware. No test traffic leaves the network.
`github.com/dshiled/qa-robot` - AGPL-3.0

### Sight and Vision Hospital OS

Lead architect and developer, 2026. Offline-first hospital operating system for
a private eye hospital, replacing paper-heavy workflows: patient records,
reception and triage, clinical workflows, pharmacy and optical operations,
billing reconciliation, inventory, and AI-assisted digitization of paper
records. Built for environments where internet connectivity cannot be assumed,
with role-based access control and cryptographically chained audit records.
Private.

### Market-systems research

Single-position, session-based strategies with explicit daily and total
drawdown halts, measured against a locked holdout and a Monte-Carlo gate. The
windows that failed are documented next to the ones that passed. Private, in
progress.

## How I work

- **Self-hosted first.** No test data and no market data leaves the box.
- **Measured claims only.** Holdout and Monte-Carlo, and the failures ship in
  the README, not just the passing numbers.
- **Append-only evidence** over dashboards and screenshots.
- **Deterministic behavior** around critical operations, with clear system
  boundaries and tenant isolation.
- **Small dependency surface.** Node built-ins and SQLite, no Docker, no cloud
  account, no database server.
- **Practical AI** rather than AI for its own sake.
